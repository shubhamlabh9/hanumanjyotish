How to Use Hanuman Jyotish

Step-by-step guide to get your predictions

1

Open the Prediction Wheel

Navigate to the Prediction Wheel page from the home page. You will see six category options available for selection.

💡 Tip: Each category has unique predictions and outcomes.

2

Select Your Category

Choose from the following categories:

  • Travel: Journey predictions
  • Business: Career growth
  • Marriage: Relationships
  • Job: Professional life
  • Health: Wellness
  • Education: Learning
3

Spin the Wheel

Click the prominent "SPIN" button in the center of the wheel. The wheel will begin rotating smoothly with a random selection algorithm.

⚡ Note: The wheel spins for approximately 4 seconds before revealing your result.

4

Read Your Prediction

Once the wheel stops, your prediction will be displayed. You will see:

  • ✓ Selected Name: The name chosen from the wheel
  • ✓ Prediction Text: Detailed information about your prediction
  • ✓ Result Status: Good, Moderate, or Difficult
  • ✓ Category Information: Details about the selected category
5

Share or Spin Again

After viewing your prediction, you have three options:

📤 Share

Share your prediction on social media or via link

🔄 Spin Again

Get another prediction from the same category

🏠 Go Home

Return to the home page to select a different category

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the predictions accurate?

The predictions are based on the traditional Hanuman Jyotish system. While they provide spiritual guidance, they should be taken as entertainment and guidance rather than definitive predictions. Your actions and choices ultimately shape your future.

Can I spin multiple times?

Yes! You can spin the wheel as many times as you like. Each spin will give you a new prediction. You can select the same category multiple times or choose different categories to explore different areas of your life.

How are the names selected?

The names are randomly selected using a computer algorithm. Each spin generates a random selection from the available names for the chosen category, ensuring fairness and unpredictability.

What do the color results mean?

Results are color-coded for easy understanding:
🟢 Green (Good): Positive outcomes expected
🟠 Orange (Moderate): Mixed results with efforts needed
🔴 Red (Difficult): Challenging times ahead, but overcome able

Is my data saved?

Currently, Hanuman Jyotish does not save your predictions. Each session is independent. Your predictions are only visible during your current browser session.
